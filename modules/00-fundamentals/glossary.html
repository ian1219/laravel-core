Words people mix up

Authentication vs authorization

🏠 Real life
🏨

At a hotel, the front desk checks your ID (authentication). Your key card then opens only your room (authorization).

=
💻 In code

        // Who are you? (must be logged in)
        Route::get('/posts/{post}/edit', [PostController::class, 'edit'])
            ->middleware('auth')
            // Are you allowed? (must own the post)
            ->can('update', 'post');
Remember it like this

Authentication = who you are. Authorization = what you're allowed. One checks your face, the other checks your key.

❌ Avoid 

          $user->password = $request->password;

Storing a password as plain text means a single leak exposes every account.

✅ Do 

          $user->password = Hash::make($request->password);

Hash it, so even you can't read it. (Laravel's default User model does this for you with a 'hashed' cast.)

Key takeaways

Big words, small ideas

  • Route → Controller → Model → View is the path almost every page takes.
  • Migrations build tables, models use them and seeders/factories fill them.
  • The service container builds objects for you, and dependency injection is how you ask for them.
  • Authentication is who you are; authorization is what you may do.
  • You don't need to memorise this page. Come back whenever a word feels scary.
